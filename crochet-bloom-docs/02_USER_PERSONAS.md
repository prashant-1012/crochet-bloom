# 02 — User Personas

## Primary: "Gifting Gia" — the occasion shopper

- Shopping for a birthday, anniversary, "just because," or apology
  gift for a partner, friend, or family member.
- Active on Instagram, discovers the brand via a shared reel/post or a
  friend's recommendation, not organic search.
- Values that the item is **handmade and won't wilt** — an
  everlasting alternative to real flowers, with a personal/thoughtful
  feel a generic gift lacks.
- Buys on mobile, often on a time constraint (an occasion is
  tomorrow), so needs fast browsing and a frictionless path to
  ordering.
- **Needs from the site**: fast trust signals (real photos of the
  actual craftsmanship, warm testimonials), clear pricing, an
  effortless way to finalize an order via WhatsApp without creating an
  account.

## Secondary: "Treat-Yourself Tara"

- Buying for herself — a bouquet or keychain as a small self-care
  purchase, not a formal "gift."
- Browses the full catalog rather than heading straight to checkout;
  cares about variety (colors, flower types) and enjoys the browsing
  experience itself.
- **Needs from the site**: an inviting, well-organized `/shop` grid
  she can browse by category (Bouquets, Keychains, Hampers, Decor),
  without needing a specific gift occasion to justify the visit.

## Tertiary: "Corporate/Bulk Buyer Bala"

- Buying multiple items — for a small business's client gifting,
  wedding favors, or festival hampers.
- Price-sensitive at volume, wants to discuss bulk pricing — WhatsApp
  checkout suits this persona well since a human can adjust pricing
  and discuss customization in chat.
- **Needs from the site**: easy quantity adjustment in cart, clear
  running total, a way to see the full catalog quickly to decide what
  to bulk-order.

## Design implications

- Every persona converts through the same shallow funnel: Home/Shop →
  Add to Cart → WhatsApp. Don't add steps (accounts, multi-page
  checkout) that only make sense for a payment-gateway flow.
- Because these are **handmade, made-to-order** items (not stocked
  inventory the way toddler workbooks are), copy throughout the site
  should set expectations gently — e.g. "handcrafted to order," rather
  than implying instant same-day dispatch, without needing a formal
  lead-time UI.
- Trust-building content (Meet the Maker, Testimonials) must be
  reachable from the homepage without hunting — gifting decisions are
  often made quickly, sometimes emotionally, and a maker's personal
  story is a bigger trust lever for a handmade brand than it was for
  the reference project's toddler-education angle.
